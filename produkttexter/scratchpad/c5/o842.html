Compact binoculars are the perfect companion for those who like to spend a lot of time in nature. With these binoculars you can easily spot details that are usually not visible to the naked eye. Focus Outdoor II has an ergonomic and lightweight design with a comfortable rubberized grip that makes it easy to hold the binoculars even when wearing gloves. Its compact size makes it comfortable to both carry and view for extended periods of time.

The binoculars come with a wide field of view that makes it easy to observe birds or other moving animals. A large, smooth focusing wheel makes it easy to find and focus quickly. Despite their low weight, the binoculars have a rugged construction that makes them waterproof and nitrogen-filled. This construction prevents fogging of the lenses when you get out of the car on a cold winter's day. The lenses have Muli Coating and the BK-7 prisms are made of optical glass, which means you get an image with true-to-life color reproduction with less distortion and minimizes strong reflections such as glare on the water surface.

  • Lightweight
  • Easy to bring on a hike
  • Great optics
  • Robust and waterproof construction

Focus Outdoor II come with a protective bag and a comfortable neck strap.

All Focus Sport Optics binoculars and spoting scopes come with a 10 year warranty.
